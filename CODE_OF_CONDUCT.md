# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming,
harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the addon.
- No harassment, insults, or discriminatory behavior of any kind.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Reports can be sent to the repository owner via GitHub.
